About

Communications that carry weight, and strategy that can be measured.

My work sits between public service and modern marketing. On one side, migration safety, public awareness and stakeholder engagement — careful, accurate, accountable communication for institutions. On the other, digital strategy for brands that need clarity, consistency and results.

I bring research discipline from international affairs into every campaign: who the audience actually is, what they need to hear, and how we will know it worked. I write, design, plan and report — and I do it in both English and French.

I am passionate about using communications to drive meaningful impact.
